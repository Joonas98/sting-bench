Sting Bench

Short musical stings for videos, podcasts, intros, notifications or games. Pick a style, then push it somewhere unexpected: a music box playing a guitar riff, a fanfare on a detuned piano. Everything is synthesized in your browser, so every take you export is yours to use.

−1.0 dBFS

−1 dBFS is full loudness. Lower it to leave headroom for your game mix.

Six takes share every setting but vary the melody, so repeated round clears don't sound identical. The wave ladder renders this take at five intensity levels, from calm to full power, so the sting can escalate as waves climb.
